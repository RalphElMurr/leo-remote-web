How to use it
  1. There's nothing to press. Leave your phone in your pocket with Bluetooth on.
  2. Walk up to the car. When your iPhone's Bluetooth connects to the car (the same link it uses for calls and music), the car unlocks.
  3. If you don't open a door within 45 seconds, it locks again by itself.
  4. With "lock when I leave" on (set on the car screen), it locks once your phone has been gone about 20 seconds.
  5. It only works while the car is awake, so turn sentry on when you park.

Notifications

–

Get a notification on your iPhone when you park (battery and location), on every scheduled wake, and for alerts. Uses the free ntfy app. Your phone page also uses these reports to show the battery and parked spot while the car sleeps.

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store and open it.
  2. Tap +, paste the topic below, and tap Subscribe. Allow notifications.
  3. Tap Turn on here, then Send test.

The topic works like a password: anyone who has it can read the reports. Reports pass through ntfy.sh and are kept there for about 12 hours.
